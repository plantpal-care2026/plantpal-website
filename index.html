THE CARE BASICS

Help your plants thrive

Five simple habits make plant care easier.

💧

Water wisely

Check the soil before watering. Use a pot with drainage and avoid leaving roots sitting in water.

☀️

Find the light

Learn whether your plant prefers bright indirect light, direct sun, or lower-light conditions.

🪴

Choose good soil

Use a suitable potting mix and a container with drainage holes to support healthy roots.

✂️

Trim gently

Remove dead or damaged leaves with clean tools and check leaves regularly for changes.

🌱

Feed thoughtfully

Use a suitable fertiliser during active growth and follow the product directions.

🔎

Observe often

Look for yellowing, pests, or drooping. Adjust care gradually instead of changing everything at once.
